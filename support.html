Questmark Support

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Questmark is a manual completion tracker for Final Fantasy X and Elden Ring, made by Durable IT Solutions. If something isn't working, or a checklist is missing something it should have, please get in touch — it's a small project and reports genuinely do get read.

Contact

Email: DuraITSolutions@pm.me

We aim to reply within a few days. There is no phone support.

You can also reach this address from inside the app: open About (the link at the bottom of the Playthroughs screen), then Contact or Report a bug. The bug-report option fills in your app version automatically, which saves a step.

Reporting a problem

The more of this you can include, the faster it can be fixed:

  • Your device and OS version (for example, iPhone 15 / iOS 18, or Pixel 8 / Android 15)
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of the Playthroughs screen and on the About screen
  • Which game and which screen you were on
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Questmark has no accounts and no server, so your playthroughs are never uploaded anywhere. The flip side is that uninstalling the app deletes your progress permanently, and it cannot be recovered afterwards.

Can I sync between two devices?

Not currently. Progress on your phone and progress on a tablet are separate, and there is no way to transfer one to the other.

Can I track more than one playthrough?

Yes. Tap + New on the Playthroughs screen to add another. Each one keeps its own progress, so a fresh run and a completionist run stay separate. Playthroughs can be renamed with the pencil icon and removed with the bin icon.

Something in a checklist is missing or wrong.

Please email us with the game, the section, and what should change. Corrections to the game data are the most useful reports we get.

Why do I see ads?

Questmark is free, and ads are what cover its costs. There is currently no paid ad-free option. If you are in a region where consent is required, you can review your ad choices from the About screen.

Is this an official Square Enix or FromSoftware app?

No. Questmark is an unofficial, fan-made tracker. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix, FromSoftware, or Bandai Namco, and all game names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
