Privacy Policy for Questmark

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Questmark ("the app") is developed by Durable IT Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. Questmark does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts and does not require you to sign in.

Data stored on your device

The progress you track (your playthroughs and checked-off items) is saved only in local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Analytics, ads, and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. No usage data is gathered. The app requests internet access only to open external links (such as the project page) in your browser; it does not send your data anywhere.

Children

Questmark is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, if a future version introduces advertising), we will update this page and its "last updated" date.
